01 — Bio

Bio

I’m a documentary director drawn to spirituality and to stories of becoming — what happens when the old life falls away and something truer asks to be lived.

My work sits with consciousness, healing, queer identity, and the search for the sacred in ordinary and extreme places. I direct from an inner vision, then go find that image in the world — and let what I film change me in return.

MFA in Digital Entrepreneurial Cinema. Short film premiered at Slamdance.
