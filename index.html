Bio

I bring ideas to life through the lens of a filmmaker and the precision of a producer.

With over 13 years in video production, I have built my career at the intersection of cinematic storytelling and scalable content operations. My foundation in film - including an MFA from the University of Central Florida and a Slamdance-selected short - informs everything I create.

For EdTech clients, I have led end-to-end production for 60+ educational courses, transforming raw instructor recordings into polished, globally-distributed content. By pioneering AI-assisted workflows, I reduced production time by 20% while maintaining the editorial standards that helped grow YouTube channels by over 400%.

Today, I partner with EdTech and technology-driven organizations to scale their video operations, blending my film training with modern tools like Runway, Descript, and ElevenLabs to deliver content that is both beautiful and efficient to produce.
