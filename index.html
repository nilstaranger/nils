01 — Bio

Bio

Documentary filmmaker by craft — director of personal features and shorts, producer of courses and brand films that meet people where they are.

During a live shoot for my first documentary, the man I was interviewing said that Castle Otttis — the castle we were filming in — was created as a place of peace for all people, including gay people. I couldn’t say anything out loud that day, but I later returned to the castle to film my admission to him: I was gay, and I needed a place of peace.

It took time to unravel my beliefs and set out after the big questions, but I filmed that too — A Blue Flower — so others could follow along and know they weren’t alone. That kind of personal documentary isn’t the only style I love, but it still shapes how I listen on set. These days I direct film work and produce educational video — including Vue Mastery projects that weave generative AI into the craft.
